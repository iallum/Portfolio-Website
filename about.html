Fun Facts

  • You’ll frequently catch me with earbuds in, listening to some music. A few of my favorites are Glass Animals, Griff, and Charlotte Cardin. And live concerts are always a blast!
  • I fenced (saber) competitively from childhood through college. Over the years, I even got to fence a few Olympians in tournament which was very cool (no, I did not win againt them).
  • While it sometimes might feel a bit of a chore mid-work week, I do love cooking and baking, especially when I’m trying an exciting new recipe. Cantonese steamed fish is my go to for when I’m trying to impress though.
  • I grew up in DC, but spent the last few years in Chicago for college and work. I’m happy to be back on the East coast in New York now though.
  • I’m a fan of arts and crafts projects, and recently I learned how to crochet. I haven’t made anything too practical yet though, just a few large plushies.
  • As much as I love listening to music, I don’t play myself. However, I did get to learn an unusual instrument in middle school. For a year, I learned to ring the giant bells in the National Cathedral which was definitely a unique experience. Hearing bells always reminds me of that fun time!
